education, health, housing, jobs, and safety.

relevant. Missouri's family courts serve thousands of families annually,
and technology solutions like CoTrackPro can meaningfully improve
outcomes for children and families.

goals. I have enclosed a one-page overview for your reference.

Thank you for your service in the General Assembly and your commitment
to innovation in public policy.
