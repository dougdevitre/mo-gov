## CAMPAIGN-SPECIFIC DATA HOOKS

Use these Missouri-specific angles when customizing one-pagers:

### Access To Justice
- Missouri has 114 counties + St. Louis City — many with limited legal aid
- Legal Services of Missouri and Legal Aid of Western Missouri serve the state
- Pro se litigants in family court face documentation challenges
- Missouri courts are modernizing with Show-Me Courts / Case.net

### Access To Education
- Missouri DESE oversees 500+ school districts
- IEP/504 navigation is a common pain point for Missouri families
- School counselor-to-student ratios vary widely by district
- K-12 safety reporting requirements under RSMo 167.117, 210.115

### Access To Health
- Missouri Medicaid (MO HealthNet) serves 1M+ enrollees
- SDOH frameworks increasingly adopted by MO HealthNet managed care
- Community health workers recognized under Missouri state programs
- Behavioral health access gaps especially in rural Missouri

### Access To Jobs
- Missouri WIOA Combined State Plan PY 2024-2027
- 14 local workforce development areas across the state
- Missouri Job Centers serve all 114 counties
- Target populations: dislocated workers, veterans, reentry, youth, SNAP/TANF

### Access To Housing
- Missouri Housing Development Commission (MHDC) programs
- Eviction prevention programs administered through community action agencies
- Fair Housing enforcement through Missouri Commission on Human Rights
- Housing instability correlated with family court outcomes

### Access To Safety
- Missouri ranks among states with highest DV rates
- Missouri Coalition Against Domestic and Sexual Violence (MCADSV)
- Adult/child protective orders under RSMo 455
- Trauma-informed documentation supports judicial accuracy

## DESIGN SPECIFICATIONS (for printed one-pagers)

- **Size**: US Letter (8.5" x 11"), single-sided
- **Margins**: 0.75" all sides
- **Font**: Clean sans-serif (Helvetica, Arial, or Inter)
- **Header**: Bold, 18-24pt
- **Body**: 11-12pt
- **Color palette**: Use CoTrackPro brand colors or neutral professional tones
- **QR code**: Link to cotrackpro.com or relevant Access To project page
- **Word count**: 250-350 words maximum — must be scannable in 30 seconds
