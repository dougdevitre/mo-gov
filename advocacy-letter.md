## VARIANT: COTRACKPRO INTRODUCTION LETTER

```
{{date}}

The Honorable {{full_name}}
Missouri {{chamber}}
201 W. Capitol Ave., Rm. {{room_number}}
Jefferson City, MO 65101

Dear {{salutation}} {{last_name}}:

My name is Doug Devitre, founder and CEO of CoTrackPro, a child-centered
documentation and safety platform based in the St. Louis area. I am
writing to introduce our work and request the opportunity to share how
CoTrackPro supports Missouri families navigating co-parenting and family
court proceedings.

Missouri's family courts serve thousands of families each year, many of
whom struggle to maintain organized, court-ready documentation while
managing the emotional complexity of custody and co-parenting disputes.
Professionals who support these families — attorneys, guardians ad litem,
mediators, therapists, and school counselors — often lack coordinated
tools to document concerns and track patterns over time.

CoTrackPro provides a trauma-informed documentation platform that helps
families and professionals organize incidents, communications, and
evidence in a structured, court-neutral format. The platform is designed
to prioritize children's safety and wellbeing while supporting the
professionals who serve them. We also maintain a family of open civic
technology projects — the "Access To" initiative — covering justice,
education, health, housing, jobs, and safety.

I would welcome the opportunity to provide a brief demonstration for you
or your staff. I am happy to visit your Capitol office or schedule a
virtual meeting at your convenience. I have enclosed a one-page overview
for your reference.

Thank you for your dedication to Missouri families and for your service
in the General Assembly.

Respectfully,

Doug Devitre
Founder & CEO, CoTrackPro
(314) 496-5973 | dougdevitre@gmail.com
cotrackpro.com
```

## TONE CHECKLIST

Before sending any letter, verify:

- [ ] Non-partisan — no mention of party affiliation
- [ ] Child-centered — connects back to children/families when relevant
- [ ] Trauma-informed — language is calm, not inflammatory
- [ ] Missouri-specific — references MO data, statutes, or programs
- [ ] Concrete ask — specific, actionable request
- [ ] One page — 350-450 words maximum
- [ ] Proper title — "The Honorable" + correct Senator/Representative
- [ ] Correct district — verified against official directory
- [ ] Contact info — full sender information included
- [ ] Enclosure note — if including additional materials
