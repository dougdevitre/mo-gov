# Advocacy Letter Template
## Missouri Legislative Outreach

---

## STANDARD LETTER FORMAT

```
[DATE]

The Honorable [FULL_NAME]
Missouri [STATE_SENATE / HOUSE_OF_REPRESENTATIVES]
201 W. Capitol Ave., Rm. [ROOM_NUMBER]
Jefferson City, MO 65101

Dear [SENATOR / REPRESENTATIVE] [LAST_NAME]:

[PARAGRAPH 1 — INTRODUCTION]
My name is [SENDER_NAME], [TITLE] of [ORGANIZATION], based in
[CITY], Missouri. I am writing to [REQUEST: bring to your attention /
request a meeting / share information about] [TOPIC] — a [BRIEF
DESCRIPTION] that directly impacts Missouri families [in your district /
across the state].

[PARAGRAPH 2 — THE PROBLEM]
[Missouri-specific data about the problem. Example frameworks:]
- "Each year, Missouri family courts process approximately [X] custody
  and visitation cases. Many families navigating these proceedings lack
  access to [specific gap]."
- "According to [SOURCE], [X%] of Missouri children are affected by
  [issue]. Current systems for [process] rely on [outdated method]."
- "[Missouri-specific statistic or trend that connects to the solution]."

[PARAGRAPH 3 — THE SOLUTION]
[ORGANIZATION / PRODUCT] is a [TYPE: platform / tool / initiative] that
[WHAT IT DOES in plain language]. It serves [WHO: co-parents, family law
attorneys, school counselors, etc.] by providing [KEY CAPABILITIES].
[PRODUCT] is designed to be [child-centered / trauma-informed /
court-ready / accessible] and [DIFFERENTIATOR].

[PARAGRAPH 4 — THE ASK]
I respectfully request [SPECIFIC ASK]:
- "...the opportunity to provide a brief demonstration for you or your
  staff at your convenience."
- "...your consideration of [TOPIC] as the [COMMITTEE] reviews
  legislation related to [SUBJECT AREA]."
- "...your support for [INITIATIVE / BILL / PILOT PROGRAM]."
- "...a 15-minute meeting to discuss how [PRODUCT] could support
  [STATE PROGRAM / INITIATIVE]."

[PARAGRAPH 5 — CLOSING]
I have enclosed [a one-page overview / additional materials] for your
reference. I am available at your convenience and would welcome the
opportunity to discuss this further. Thank you for your service to
Missouri families [and to the residents of District [NUMBER]].

Respectfully,

[SENDER_NAME]
[TITLE]
[ORGANIZATION]
[PHONE] | [EMAIL]
[WEBSITE]
```

Missouri's family courts serve thousands of families each year, many of

managing the emotional complexity of custody and co-parenting disputes.

tools to document concerns and track patterns over time.

CoTrackPro provides a trauma-informed documentation platform that helps
families and professionals organize incidents, communications, and
evidence in a structured, court-neutral format. The platform is designed

professionals who serve them. We also maintain a family of open civic
technology projects — the "Access To" initiative — covering justice,
education, health, housing, jobs, and safety.

in the General Assembly.

## TONE CHECKLIST

Before sending any letter, verify:

- [ ] Non-partisan — no mention of party affiliation
- [ ] Child-centered — connects back to children/families when relevant
- [ ] Trauma-informed — language is calm, not inflammatory
- [ ] Missouri-specific — references MO data, statutes, or programs
- [ ] Concrete ask — specific, actionable request
- [ ] One page — 350-450 words maximum
- [ ] Proper title — "The Honorable" + correct Senator/Representative
- [ ] Correct district — verified against official directory
- [ ] Contact info — full sender information included
- [ ] Enclosure note — if including additional materials
