# Capitol Drop-Off Packet Template
## Assembly & Delivery Checklist

> **Last verified**: April 2026
> **Template variable syntax**: `{{variable_name}}`

---

## PACKET CONTENTS (per legislator)

### Required
- [ ] **Cover letter** — personalized with legislator name, district, committee hook
- [ ] **One-pager / policy brief** — printed single-sided, color preferred
- [ ] **Folder or large envelope** — labeled: "For the Office of [Title] [Last Name]"

### Recommended
- [ ] **Business card** — 2 copies (one for the front desk, one for the legislator)
- [ ] **Product overview card** — 5x7 or half-sheet with QR code to cotrackpro.com
- [ ] **USB drive or link card** — with demo video or additional materials (optional)

### Do NOT include
- ✗ Anything that could be perceived as a gift (food, branded merchandise, etc.)
  — Missouri ethics rules restrict gifts to legislators
- ✗ Anything requiring a signature or immediate response
- ✗ Excessively bulky materials — keep it professional and portable

## DELIVERY SCRIPT (what to say at each office)

### If the legislator IS available:
```
"Good [morning/afternoon], [Senator/Representative] [Last Name].
My name is Doug Devitre. I'm the founder of CoTrackPro — a
child-centered documentation platform for families in the court
system. I wanted to drop off some materials about our work and
see if there might be a time to give you a brief overview.
It's very relevant to [committee name / district issue]."
```

### If only staff is available (more common):
```
"Good [morning/afternoon]. My name is Doug Devitre with CoTrackPro.
I'd like to leave some materials for [Senator/Representative]
[Last Name] about a child-centered documentation platform we've
built for families navigating family court. Is there a good time
to schedule a brief meeting with the [Senator/Representative] or
a member of the policy staff?"
```

### Key points to convey (pick 2-3):
1. CoTrackPro is a documentation platform for families in the court system
2. It's child-centered and trauma-informed
3. It supports 35 professional roles (attorneys, GALs, mediators, therapists)
4. We also maintain open civic tech tools for justice, education, health, etc.
5. We're a Missouri-based company
6. We'd welcome 15 minutes to demonstrate the platform

### What NOT to do:
- Don't overstay — be warm but brief (2-3 minutes max per office)
- Don't argue politics or take positions on pending legislation
- Don't criticize other products, services, or legislators
- Don't leave without getting a name to follow up with
- Don't forget to log the visit

## PRE-DELIVERY PREPARATION CHECKLIST

### 1 week before
- [ ] Finalize target list (names + room numbers)
- [ ] Verify room numbers are current (call Senate/House switchboard)
- [ ] Print all cover letters (personalized)
- [ ] Print one-pagers
- [ ] Print business cards (if low)
- [ ] Order folders or large envelopes
- [ ] Prepare folder labels
- [ ] Print delivery log
- [ ] Check Capitol schedule — confirm session day

### Day before
- [ ] Assemble all packets
- [ ] Label each folder
- [ ] Organize in delivery route order (see Capitol Visit Planner)
- [ ] Charge phone (for notes and photos)
- [ ] Prepare a clipboard with delivery log
- [ ] Set calendar reminders for follow-up emails (48 hours post-visit)

### Day of
- [ ] Dress professionally (business casual minimum, suit preferred for Capitol)
- [ ] Arrive early (Capitol opens 7 AM during session)
- [ ] Start with your own district's legislator if present
- [ ] Work through target list systematically
- [ ] Log every delivery
- [ ] Collect staff names and emails where offered
