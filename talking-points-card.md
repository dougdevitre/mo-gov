## IF THEY ASK "WHAT DOES IT DO?"

- Families document incidents in structured, court-neutral formats
- Organizes communications and evidence chronologically
- Generates court-ready reports and timelines
- De-escalates co-parenting communications
- Builds safety plans grounded in documented patterns
