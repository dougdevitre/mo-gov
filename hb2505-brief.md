## WHAT THIS BILL DOES

HB 2505 gives grandparents statutory priority when a court determines
that third-party placement (rather than either parent) is in a child's
best interest. Currently, Missouri law allows third-party custody but
doesn't prioritize grandparents over other third parties.

## WHY IT MATTERS TO COTRACKPRO

This bill directly affects the family court proceedings CoTrackPro
was built to support:

1. **Documentation needs increase** — When grandparents seek custody,
   they need organized documentation of why they should be the preferred
   third party. CoTrackPro's structured, court-neutral format is designed
   for exactly this scenario.

2. **More professionals involved** — Third-party custody cases typically
   involve attorneys, guardians ad litem, social workers, and sometimes
   therapists. CoTrackPro supports all 35 of these professional roles
   with coordinated documentation.

3. **Child safety focus** — The bill's premise is that grandparent
   placement often serves children's best interests. CoTrackPro's
   child-centered design aligns with this principle.

## QUICK FACTS FOR ANY CONVERSATION

- HB 2505 **passed the House** and is now before your committee
- The bill gives grandparents **priority, not automatic custody** —
  courts still decide based on best interests
- Missouri already allows third-party custody; this bill adds a
  **preference framework** within that existing structure
- CoTrackPro can help families on **both sides** of these cases
  organize their documentation
